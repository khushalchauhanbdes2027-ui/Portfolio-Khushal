About

From Pixels to Perspective.

I’m Khushal Chauhan, a 3D Environment, Modelling and Texturing Artist currently studying Animation and VFX at ATLAS SKILLTECH UNIVERSITY. I enjoy turning ideas into detailed 3D worlds, from modelling and texturing individual assets to building environments that have their own sense of place and atmosphere. I’m always experimenting, learning, and finding better ways to bring an idea from my head onto the screen.

Alongside 3D art, I’ve been passionate about photography since childhood. I got my first DSLR in 2025 and have been continuously developing my photography skills alongside my studies. I also worked as an editor for several YouTubers, giving me an early experience in visual storytelling and content creation.
